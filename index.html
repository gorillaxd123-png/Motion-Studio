Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Motion Studio.

How does the 60 FPS Canvas recording work?

Motion Studio uses hardware-accelerated MediaStream API to capture high-definition real-time animations straight out of your browser canvas without backend rendering delays.

How do the 5 Image Slots work in Showcase mode?

Upload up to 5 custom images into the right Inspector media slots. Selecting Showcase will sequence and pan across all 5 images smoothly with real-time motion blur.
